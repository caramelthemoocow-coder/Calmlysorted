Decluttering & household help · Leeds

When life feels a lot,
I’m here to lend a hand.

Life can sometimes feel overwhelming, and keeping on top of your home can become one more thing to manage.

I’m Jane. I offer decluttering, organising and practical household help for busy households and anyone finding day-to-day life difficult. Whether you’re juggling family life, dealing with illness or simply don’t know where to start, I can work alongside you to make things feel more manageable.

There’s no need to tidy up before asking for help. We can start small, at your pace, without judgement.

How I can help

Support for your space and your next step.

01

Decluttering

I can help you work through the things that have built up, from a drawer or cupboard to a wardrobe or room. You decide what stays, and we take it one step at a time.

02

Organising

I can help you sort paperwork, organise cupboards and give your belongings a practical home, with simple arrangements that work for your everyday life.

03

Body doubling

Sometimes it helps to have someone alongside you. I can be there while you tackle a task, offering company and encouragement as you get started and keep going.

04

A helping hand at home

For busy parents and households where everyday tasks are piling up, I can help with toy tidy-ups, cupboard clearing and those little jobs that make home feel easier to manage.

05

During or after illness

When your energy needs to go towards feeling better, I can offer practical help with sorting, organising and household tasks. We can talk about what would help most and take things gently.
